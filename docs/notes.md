## how wikipeida deals with disputes

[article](https://wikimediafoundation.org/news/2025/11/10/how-wikipedia-navigates-disputes/)

* A single editor on Wikipedia cannot unilaterally settle a content dispute, particularly on a contentious topic.

* disagreements happen on talk pages that are pubic
* editors can use a structured process called a “Request for Comment (RfC)”. This helps flag the debate for more members of the Wikipedia community to weigh in on the outcome
  * RfCs also provide a record for past decisions, so that future decisions can build on the discussions and evaluation that came before it.
